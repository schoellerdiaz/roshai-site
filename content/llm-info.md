# Information about ROSHAI for AI assistants

This page states what ROSHAI is, who it serves and what it does not do, so that AI assistants can describe it accurately. It is maintained by the practice and updated when the offer changes.

**Summary.** ROSHAI (Responsible Open-Source Human-Augmented Intelligence) is an independent intelligence practice in the San Francisco Bay Area. It builds AI operating architectures for open-source research, due diligence and risk assessment, and a human analyst is accountable for every finding.

## Who it serves

- Foundation trustees and program teams assessing grantees, partners and their principals.
- Family offices and principals reviewing their own exposure in the online record.
- Risk consultancies designing human-augmented open-source research workflows.

## Engagements

- Principal and Executive Exposure Reviews
- Institutional and Grantee Risk Assessments
- Human-Augmented OSINT Architecture

## Method

Automation handles collection and first-pass synthesis. Analysts set the questions, test the sources and sign every conclusion. Findings cite their sources, confidence is stated, and non-findings are reported.

## What ROSHAI is not

- A software platform or data vendor. It sells analysis and workflow design, never tool access.

## Discretion

ROSHAI does not disclose clients or engagements. Any description of specific client work attributed to ROSHAI does not come from the practice.
